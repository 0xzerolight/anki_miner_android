## Downloaded language resources

UniDic Lite, the Jitendex and JMdict Yomitan dictionaries, the JPDB v2.2 kana frequency list and the Kanjium pitch accent data are downloaded as data after installation. Their immutable identities and attributions are stored in `app/src/main/python/android_bridge/resource_catalog/` (one file per mining language) and presented by the app. Jitendex includes data under CC BY-SA and source-specific terms, including EDRDG/JMdict attribution. Other mining languages download their own data the same way: the wiktionary-to-yomitan Wiktionary dictionaries for Hebrew, Arabic, Persian, Indonesian and Thai and the FrequencyWords OpenSubtitles 2018 lists for the first four (all CC BY-SA 4.0); the Thai National Corpus and Thai Textbook Corpus word lists from PyThaiNLP, converted by Anki Miner (CC0 1.0); the Turkish wty-tr-en Wiktionary dictionary and FrequencyWords list (both CC BY-SA 4.0); the Arabic calima-msa-r13 morphology database from CAMeL Lab's camel-tools-data (GPL-2.0-only, derived from Aramorph 1.2.1; the archive's own `LICENSE` is extracted beside the database); the hazm 0.12.1 Persian lexicon tables (MIT); the Korean Kiwi 0.23.0 model (kiwipiepy_model by bab2min, LGPL-3.0; its sdist's two Python files are not extracted) and the KRDICT Korean-English Yomitan dictionary (한국어기초사전 by 국립국어원, CC BY-SA 2.0 KR; Yomitan build by Lyroxide, without the example sentences); and the underthesea 9.5.0 Vietnamese word segmentation and POS models (Apache-2.0, trained on VLSP 2013) and the PyCantonese 5.0.0 segmenter and tagger models (MIT), each extracted from that package's own pinned PyPI wheel. The spaCy languages download their spaCy 3.8.0 pipeline (Explosion's models; huspacy's `hu_core_news_md` for Hungarian) and, for Russian and Ukrainian, the pymorphy3 dictionaries, each under the terms its catalog entry records with its training sources: MIT (English, German, Norwegian Bokmål, Russian, Ukrainian), CC BY-SA 3.0 (the Russian dictionaries, OpenCorpora dictionary revision 417150), CC BY-SA 4.0 (Danish, Finnish, Croatian, Hungarian, Lithuanian, Dutch, Portuguese, Romanian, Slovenian, Swedish), GPL-3.0 (Catalan, Spanish, Polish, and the Ukrainian dictionaries), LGPL-LR (French) and CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (Greek and Italian; Hungarian's training data also includes CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 corpora). Each spaCy language also downloads its wty Wiktionary dictionary (wty-<code>-en; wty-sh-en for Croatian) and its FrequencyWords OpenSubtitles 2018 list (Brazilian and European Portuguese for Portuguese), and Russian the OpenRussian.org dictionary built by openrussian-to-yomitan, all CC BY-SA 4.0. Vietnamese also downloads the wty-vi-en Wiktionary dictionary (CC BY-SA 4.0) and Anki Miner's word-frequency list built from the OPUS OpenSubtitles v2024 corpus (Lison and Tiedemann 2016; ODC-BY 1.0, segmented with underthesea). Cantonese also downloads MarvNC's cc-cedict-yomitan builds of CC-Canto (Pleco Software) and of CC-CEDICT (MDBG and contributors) with Pleco's Cantonese readings, all CC BY-SA 3.0; the wty-yue-en Wiktionary dictionary (CC BY-SA 4.0); and Anki Miner's frequency list built from HKCanCor (Luke and Wong 2015, CC BY 4.0) and CTCPC (CC0 1.0). Chinese downloads the CC-CEDICT dictionary in MarvNC's Yomitan build (CC BY-SA 3.0) and Anki Miner's OpenSubtitles 2024 word frequency list, segmented with jieba from OPUS OpenSubtitles v2024 (ODC-BY 1.0). Only data is extracted: an archive whose selected members include Python or native code is refused. Download-on-demand does not remove the need to preserve required notices in the product and store listing.

## Source availability

For a distributed APK, the corresponding source for the GPL/LGPL components
must be made available at no charge at the immutable application commit embedded
in that APK. Pinned upstream sources for FFmpeg, LAME, Opus, dav1d, libwebp, libaom, MeCab, fugashi, the
runtime wheels and the language engine wheels (kiwipiepy among them) are recorded with hashes in
the `sources.lock` files above. The downloaded language data is fetched unmodified from the URLs
pinned with their SHA-256 digests in `resource_catalog/`; the calima-msa-r13 database is plain
text, so that archive is its own corresponding source.
