# Corresponding-source and relinking review

Complete this review with qualified legal input for the exact candidate. The presence of [LICENSE](../LICENSE), source code, or a public repository does not by itself prove that every distribution obligation is satisfied.

## Required package

- [ ] Project-specific license scope and GPL-3.0-or-later compatibility were reviewed.
- [ ] Exact GPL engine revision, source, modifications/overrides, build scripts, and notices are included.
- [ ] AnkiDroid API per-file terms, complete LGPL/GPL texts, modifications, application source, and practical replacement/rebuild instructions are included.
- [ ] FFmpeg/LAME/Opus exact terms were audited from locked sources; notices, source, configuration, patches, and required static-relink material are included and tested.
- [ ] MeCab/mecab_for_dart, Fugashi, Chaquopy, CPython, OpenSSL, SQLite, runtime wheels, Android/Kotlin dependencies, Unicode data, and downloaded resource attributions were reconciled against the exact artifact.
- [ ] Complete third-party notice output is accessible to recipients and archived with the release.
- [ ] Corresponding-source archive and artifact checksums are published together at a stable public URL.
- [ ] Recipient-controlled rebuild, relink, signing, and installation procedure was tested from a clean host without publisher credentials or private caches.
- [ ] Play/store terms and any EULA were reviewed for GPL/LGPL conflicts and reverse-engineering restrictions.
- [ ] Source availability/retention owner and duration are recorded.
- [ ] Legal reviewer, scope, date, open issues, and decision are recorded without claiming broader clearance.

Detailed engineering expectations are in [SOURCE_AND_RELINKING.md](../SOURCE_AND_RELINKING.md).
